The two incentives

What you can deduct

The law rewards two things: producing culture in Spain and carrying out research. These are the percentages each project generates.

Culture

Art. 36 LIS
  • Spanish films and audiovisual series Spanish-produced feature films, shorts and series. Art. 36.1
    30%on the first million · 25% on the excess
  • Foreign productions shot in Spain Production services for international shoots. Art. 36.2
    30%on the first million · 25% on the excess
  • Live performances Performing arts and music: theatre, dance, opera, concerts. Art. 36.3
    20%of direct costs · max. 500.000 €/year

R&D and Innovation

Art. 35 LIS
  • R&D expenditure for the year General base of the research and development credit
    25%
  • Excess over the average of the previous 2 years Rewards those who increase their investment effort
    42%
  • Qualified research staff Assigned exclusively to R&D activities
    +17%additional
  • Fixed assets used for R&D Tangible and intangible, excluding buildings and land
    +8%additional
  • Technological innovation Substantial improvement of existing products or processes
    12%
And here's the key

You don't have to shoot a film
or set up a lab.

You come in as a financier: you contribute money to the project and the tax credit it generates is applied in your tax return. You don't produce, manage or bear the production risk.

  • Art. 39.7 LIS Culture — film, series and live performances
  • Art. 39.3 LIS R&D&I — research and innovation projects
120% Credit cap

The credit you apply cannot exceed 1,20 times what you contribute. Hence the 120%: you get your money back plus 20% more.

50% Cap on your tax liability

The joint limit in art. 39.1 LIS is 25% of the reduced gross tax liability, and rises to 50% when the credit exceeds 10% of that liability.

Step by step

How the investment is formalised

The same mechanism in both cases, but with a different timeline. Choose yours.

Self-employed · Income tax

You contribute in 2026 and apply it in the income tax return you file in 2027.

1
Before investing

Booking and consultation

We review your case, calculate how much you can deduct and choose together the project that suits you best. We reserve your investment ticket.

2
During 2026

Signing and contribution

You sign the financing agreement and pay the amount before 31 December 2026.

3
After the contribution

Certification

The project obtains its ICAA or INAEM certificate, or the reasoned report if it is R&D, and the agreement is notified to the AEAT.

4
April – June 2027

2026 income tax return

We file your return and apply the credit directly against the tax payable.

5
Result

You recover from 120%

Your contribution comes back in full as a lower tax bill, plus at least 20% extra saving.

Companies · Corporate Income Tax

1
Before investing

Booking and consultation

2
During the 2026 tax year

Signing and contribution

Financing agreement and payment within the tax year itself, before the accounts are closed.

3
After the contribution

Certification

ICAA or INAEM certificates, or the R&D project's reasoned report, and notification of the agreement to the AEAT.

4
1 – 25 July 2027

Form 200

We file the Corporate Income Tax return for the 2026 tax year with the credit applied against the tax payable.

5
Result

You recover from 120%

The company recovers the contribution through a lower tax bill, with at least 20% extra over the amount invested.
