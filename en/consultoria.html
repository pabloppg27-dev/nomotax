Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the express opinion and the session?

The opinion is for a specific question that can be answered in writing. The session is better when you have several related questions or want to weigh up options live with the adviser.

What is the advisory session like?

By video call or phone, whichever you prefer. Before the session we review the documents you send us to make the most of the hour, and afterwards we send you a written summary of what we covered and the next steps.

Can the price change?

The prices you see are a starting point. Before we start we review your case and confirm the final price in writing. If we see it needs more work than expected, we tell you first and you decide.

I have received a notice from the Tax Agency. Is it urgent?

Usually, yes. To answer an information request or object to a proposed assessment you normally have 10 working days, and to appeal an assessment, one month. Deadlines run from the day after notification, so write to us as soon as possible.

What documents do I need to prepare?

It depends on the case: your latest income tax return, deeds, exchange statements, the Tax Agency notice… Once we receive your request we'll tell you exactly what we need, without asking for anything extra.

What if I don't know which service I need?

Ask for a free consultation. We look at your case in a first call and recommend the format that fits, or tell you if you don't need one at all.
