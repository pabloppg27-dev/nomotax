Cookie policy

Legal documents Legal notice Privacy policy Cookie policy

This policy explains what cookies are and how they are used on this website, in accordance with article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI-CE) and data protection law.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser when you visit it. They are used, for example, to make the site work properly, remember your preferences or measure how it is used.

2. Cookies used on this site

This website does not use its own analytics or advertising cookies. Only technical cookies strictly necessary for the site to work, or for the hosting server's security, could be set; these are exempt from consent.

The only third-party tool embedded is the Calendlycalendar, on the free consultationpage, which lets you book your appointment. When that page loads, Calendly may set its own cookies, needed for the calendar to work and others according to its cookie policy.

Analytics Measuring the number of visits and how the site is used. Yes No

Third-party Features of external services embedded in the site. Depends on the purpose Only Calendly, on the free consultation page

3. Links to third-party services

Some buttons, such as the WhatsApp one, take you to third-party services. When you click them, those services may set their own cookies under their policies, over which NomoTax has no control.

4. How to manage cookies

You can allow, block or delete cookies in your browser settings, usually under privacy or security (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge and other common browsers allow this). If you block technical cookies, some parts of the site may not work properly.

5. Changes to this policy

If in the future we add cookies that require your consent, we will ask for it through a notice before setting them and will update this policy.
