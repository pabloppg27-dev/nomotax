Frequently asked questions

Which jurisdiction suits me?

It depends on where you live, your activity and where your clients are. If you're not sure, start with the free consultation; if you want the numbers, with the tax study.

Do I have to travel to incorporate the company?

In most cases, no: we handle everything remotely. If a country requires you to be present, we tell you before we start.

What is eIDAS?

It's the European system that lets you use your national electronic identity, such as your ID card or electronic certificate, for procedures in other EU countries. With it you sign the incorporation without travelling.

How long does it take?

It depends on the country: in the United Kingdom, one day; an EU company from home, a few days. Before we start we give you the exact timeframe for your jurisdiction.

Can I have a company abroad if I live in another country?

Yes, but your tax residency rules. Many countries tax their residents' foreign companies or consider that a company is resident where it is managed. That's why we start with your case.

Who handles the bookkeeping and taxes?

We do. With annual compliance we take care of your company's bookkeeping, returns and obligations every year.
